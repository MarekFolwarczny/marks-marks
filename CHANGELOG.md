# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Initial public release

- Text notes with automatic saving, separate folders, search, and alphabetical ordering.
- Floating transparent logo, system tray access, and Ctrl+Alt+N shortcut.
- Indented sub-bullets with Tab.
- Clipboard images and local video attachments with viewers, zoom, navigation,
  renaming, and confirmed deletion.
- Notes and attachments transferred together through ZIP export/import.
- English and Czech interface languages, with English as the default.
- Dark metallic theme, custom scrollbars, and two-line note group names.
- Per-file backups and recovery for interrupted project/attachment operations.
- Standalone Windows executable with embedded graphics.
